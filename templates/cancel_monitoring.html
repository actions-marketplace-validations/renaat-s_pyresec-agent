Cancel Continuous Monitoring

Secure, no-account cancellation — NanoClone Life Sciences Ltd.

We don't hide the cancel button behind a support ticket. You can cancel your PYRESEC Continuous Monitoring subscription at any time and it stops at your next billing cycle.

Option 1 — Email (fastest, no login required)

Send an email from the address you used at Stripe checkout to info@nanoclonesystems.com with the subject CANCEL MONITORING.

We use the sender address as proof of ownership and confirm cancellation within one business day. You keep access until the current paid period ends.

Option 2 — Stripe Customer Portal

If you received a Stripe invoice or receipt, you can also manage or cancel the subscription directly in your Stripe billing portal.

If you cannot find the link, use Option 1 and we will send you a secure, one-time portal URL.

What happens after cancellation

• Your weekly scans and delta reports stop at the end of the current billing period.
• No partial-month refunds for the current period unless the first weekly scan has not yet run.
• All previously delivered baseline reports remain yours.
